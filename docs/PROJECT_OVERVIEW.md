## Product Priorities

1. Correct quiz behavior.
2. Secure answer keys.
3. Reliable autosave.
4. Immutable historical attempts.
5. Server-side scoring.
6. Flexible Quiz Builder.
7. Manual essay grading.
8. Mobile-first sales experience.
9. RLS-first authorization.
10. Maintainable architecture.
